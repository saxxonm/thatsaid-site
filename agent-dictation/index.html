  • Front window only. The screenshot shows just the window you're using, not your whole screen.

Works with your agent

You review it. You send it.

Agent Dictation never sends anything on its own. When you let go of the keys, a review panel opens with your words and the Attach screenshot box. Nothing goes out until you've reviewed it and pressed Return. Press Esc and nothing is sent.

  • Transcribed on your Mac. Your voice is transcribed on-device with NVIDIA Parakeet. Audio never leaves your Mac.
  • Only what you approve goes. Just the reviewed text, plus the screenshot if the box is checked, goes to the agent you picked. The screenshot is handed over as a local file path.
  • Front window only. A screenshot shows only the window in front, never your whole screen.
  • Saved only when you send. A screenshot is kept only if you send it, in thatsaid's own folder.
  • Deleted on a schedule. Saved screenshots are deleted after 7 days, or sooner if the folder passes 200 MB. Clear now in Settings removes them at any time.
  • Never on your clipboard. The screenshot isn't put on your clipboard.
  • Starts unchecked where it matters. The Attach screenshot box starts unchecked in 1Password, Messages and any app you add, and during meetings.
  • Password fields turn it off. Whenever a password field is focused, screenshots are always off.

Three ways to use your voice

  • Dictation. Talk instead of type, anywhere on your Mac, with fn or an fn combo you choose.
  • Agent Dictation. Talk to your agent with the window you're on, on its own shortcut: Right Option + Right Command by default. thatsaid won't let the two shortcuts overlap.
  • Meetings. thatsaid records meetings hands-free and transcribes them on your device, and your agents can read them through MCP.

Questions

What is Agent Dictation?

Agent Dictation lets you speak a request to your AI agent from anywhere on your Mac. Hold your key combo, talk, review the text in a panel, and press Return to send it, with or without a screenshot of the front window.

Which agents does it work with?

Grok Bot and OpenBot, more coming. With Grok Bot, the message arrives prefilled and you press Return there to send it. With OpenBot, it goes straight into that agent's queue with no second Return; you turn on OpenBot's Local scripts for that agent once first.

What's the shortcut, and is it the same as dictation?

Agent Dictation defaults to Right Option + Right Command, and you can choose your own combo. Regular dictation uses fn or an fn combo. They're separate shortcuts, and thatsaid won't let them overlap.

Does anything get sent without me?

No. When you let go of the keys, thatsaid transcribes what you said and opens a review panel. You can edit the text and keep or uncheck the screenshot. Press Return to send or Esc to cancel. Nothing goes out until you confirm.

Where does my voice go?

It stays on your Mac. Transcription happens on-device with NVIDIA Parakeet, and audio never leaves your Mac. Only the reviewed text and, if the box is checked, the screenshot go to the agent you picked. The screenshot goes as a local file path.

What does the screenshot include, and where is it kept?

Only the front window. It's saved only when you send, in thatsaid's own folder, and deleted after 7 days, or sooner if the folder passes 200 MB. You can clear it any time with Clear now in Settings. It's never put on your clipboard.

When does the screenshot start unchecked?

The Attach screenshot box starts unchecked in apps like 1Password and Messages, in any app you add, and during meetings. Screenshots are always off when a password field is focused.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later. Agent Dictation isn't on iPhone or Apple Watch.
