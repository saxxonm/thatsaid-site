Illustrative

Everything you’d want on your wrist.

  • All-day recording

    Start once and keep recording through a day of meetings. Long sessions go to your iPhone in parts.

  • Back on after calls

    A phone call pauses the recording. When you hang up, the mic comes back on by itself.

  • Siri and alarms

    Ask Siri to open thatsaid, then tap to start. If an alarm or Siri cuts in, thatsaid picks back up, or asks for one tap when watchOS needs it.

  • Mark a moment

    Tap the bookmark to pin what was just said. It lands at that spot in the transcript.

  • Complications

    A thatsaid complication on your watch face and in the Smart Stack. Tap to open the app.

  • Shortcuts and the Action button

    A Shortcut you run or the Action button on Apple Watch Ultra opens the app, then you tap to start.

  • On-device transcription

    Your iPhone turns Watch audio into text on-device. Audio is deleted from your Watch once your iPhone has it.

Then it goes further.

  • Conversations, not noise

    thatsaid detects the conversations in a long recording, skips the silence and clears out junk recordings on its own.

  • One library everywhere

    Record on your wrist, read it on your iPhone. With encrypted cloud sync on, it reaches your Mac too.

  • Your agents can ask

    Grok Bot, Claude or ChatGPT can search what you recorded on your wrist, read-only, through the thatsaid MCP connector.

  • No API key to bring

    Transcription and summaries are built in. Nothing to paste, no model to set up.

  • Private by default

    Speech is transcribed on your devices, and no agent sees a meeting until you connect it.

  • Recording you can see

    A red ● Recording label stays on screen while the display is on, and setup walks you through consent first.

The Watch app works with thatsaid on your iPhone, which transcribes what your Watch records.

Least privilege, by default.

  • Read-only by design

    Agents can search and read transcripts. They can never edit, delete or start a recording.

  • A token per agent

    Every connection gets its own token, scoped read-only.

  • Revoke in one click

    Pull a token and that agent loses access immediately.

  • Access log

    See which agent read what, and when.

  • Encrypted cloud sync

    Cloud data is encrypted on our servers and isolated per account, behind email, password and 2FA.

  • Local when you want it

    On-device transcription, plus a Local-only mode on Mac that never syncs.
